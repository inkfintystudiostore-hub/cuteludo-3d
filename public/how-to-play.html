Game Rules

  1. Choose a Mode: Play Solo vs smart AI bots, Pass & Play locally, or share a 9-digit room code with friends.
  2. Spawn on 6: Roll a 6 on the 3D dice to move a token out of your yard.
  3. Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6 or capturing an opponent token grants an extra turn.
  4. Safe Stars: Tokens on star tiles cannot be captured.
  5. Win: First player to get all tokens into the center Home wins.
