Play Chibi Ludo 3D

Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on Chibi Ludo 3D. Please review Google's advertising disclosures:

  • For more information on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, visit Google Partner Sites Policy.

Local Device Storage

We store game preferences locally on your device using browser LocalStorage. This includes sound and music volume settings, display nicknames, local match preferences, voice chat privacy preferences, and daily spin streak timers. No sensitive personal data is stored in LocalStorage.

Authentication & Multiplayer

You can play Chibi Ludo 3D as a guest without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we store only your public account ID, chosen username, and virtual game wallet coin balance on our Cloudflare Durable Objects server to synchronize your progress across devices. We never access your contacts or private Google data.

In-Game Purchases

Optional virtual coin purchases are processed via certified payment gateways (such as Razorpay). We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank details, or UPI PINs on our servers.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to submit a data inquiry, contact us at support@inkfinitystudio.store or visit inkfinitystudio.store.
