Our Goal

Chibi Ludo 3D was built to make classic Ludo feel tactile, expressive, and effortless to start with friends. Instead of forcing a heavy app-store download, the entire 3D board, physics dice, and chibi animal squads load directly in your browser.

Key Features

  • 3D Tabletop Presentation: Custom Three.js board, animated chibi animal tokens, and 3D dice rolls.
  • Server-Authoritative Multiplayer: Online rooms run on low-latency Cloudflare Durable Objects so dice rolls and move rules stay fair for every player.
  • Social Play Built In: Free peer-to-peer voice chat with granular privacy controls, plus animated reaction emotes and room chat.
  • Flexible Modes: Solo vs. smart AI bots, local Pass & Play on one device, 2v2 Teams, and private 9-digit online rooms.
