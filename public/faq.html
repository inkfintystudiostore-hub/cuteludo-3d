Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing Chibi Ludo 3D in your browser.

Play Chibi Ludo 3D Now

Do I need to download or install an app?

No. Chibi Ludo 3D runs directly in modern mobile and desktop browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) using WebGL. You can also optionally install it as a lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA) from your browser menu for quick home-screen access.

How do I play online with friends?

Open the lobby, choose Play Online, and create a private room. You will receive a 9-digit room code and a shareable invite link. Send the code or link to up to 3 friends—if you start a match before all seats are filled, smart AI bots can fill the remaining spots.

Is voice chat free, and can I control who hears me?

Yes, peer-to-peer voice chat is completely free in online rooms. You can mute your microphone at any time, mute or block individual players, or switch Voice Privacy to Allow-Only so only players you explicitly approve can speak with you.

What happens if I roll three 6s in a row?

To keep matches fair and prevent runaway turns, rolling three consecutive 6s forfeits your third turn and passes the dice to the next player.

Are in-game coins real money?

No. Coins in Chibi Ludo 3D are virtual entertainment items used for table stakes, wheel spins, and in-game reactions. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real-world currency.

Do I need an account to play?

No—you can play immediately as a guest. Signing in with Google is optional and only used if you want to sync your username and virtual coin wallet across devices.

How can I report a bug or contact support?

Email support@inkfinitystudio.store with your browser/device details and a short description of the issue.
