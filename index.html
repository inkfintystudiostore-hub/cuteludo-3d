How to Play Chibi Ludo

Chibi Ludo 3D is a classic cross-and-circle strategy game. Roll the 3D dice, guide your animal team around the track, capture opponents' tokens, and be the first to reach the center Home!

  • Spawn on a 6: Roll a 6 to move a token from your yard onto the board track.
  • Extra Turns: Rolling a 6 or capturing an opponent token grants an instant bonus roll.
  • Safe Stars: Tokens situated on star tiles cannot be captured.
  • Game Modes: Play Solo vs smart AI bots, Pass & Play locally, or create a private 9-digit room with friends.
